# EH-in-Applied-Electric-Fields
Vlasov simulations of electron phase space holes in applied electric fields

The codes are used to conduct the plasma simulations and plot the figures in the research paper.

> R. Guo. Simulations of self-accelerating electron phase space holes in an applied electric field Phys. Plasmas, 33, 082304 (2026);\
> [doi: 10.1063/5.0342531]([https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0342531])

The paper studied the self-acceleration of electron phase space holes (EHs) in an applied electric field via one-dimensional electrostatic Vlasov simulations. The EH behaviors were investigated by applying uniform and sinusoidal electric fields, respectively. It is found that the uniform electric field applied in the direction of the self-acceleration can delay the onset of this process and change the final speed of EHs. The applied sinusoidal electric field can fix the EHs at their initial positions and suppress the self-acceleration if the electric field amplitude and duration are appropriate. In addition, it is observed that these external electric fields can induce the splitting of EHs and the generation of secondary EHs.
